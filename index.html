LetItRip_ROS

A spinning meltybrain robot that can be driven by an RC transmitter or by ROS 2. The firmware runs on a Teensy 4.0; the ROS commands arrive over an HC-12 radio.

firmware buildsnot tested on the robot yet

How it moves

The whole robot spins like a top. An accelerometer measures how fast it turns, and from that the Teensy keeps track of its angle in every rotation.

  • Heading: an LED lights up at the same angle in every turn, so the driver sees one steady arc that marks "forward".
  • Moving: for half of each turn one motor is throttled back, for the other half the other motor. The robot drifts towards the side where that happens.
  • RC or ROS: a switch on the transmitter decides who drives. In ROS mode cmd_vel sets direction and speed and weapon/speed sets the spin.
