Only if the old receiver is gone: SBUS receiver

The steering ranges in the firmware (240 to 1807) look like a FlySky receiver, so the old one may still fit. A new one has to match the transmitter, output SBUS and carry 10 channels.

The FlySky FS-iA10B is the candidate: 10 channels, about €20 to 27 on eBay.de. Check SBUS output and stock before buying, because the listings disagree.

Open points

  • 16 V limit: a 4S pack is 14.8 V nominal but 16.8 V fully charged. Check whether the limit means nominal or full charge.
  • Weight class: still open. It decides the battery size.
  • Radio power: set both HC-12 modules to AT+P4 and stay on channels 1 to 4.
