Before you solder

  • Check that your PCB routes the ESC telemetry wires to pins 0 and 7 and breaks out pins 16 and 17 for the HC-12. Those are the connections the existing code cannot confirm.
  • If an ESC has its own 5 V output (BEC), use either that or the separate regulator for the 5 V rail, not both.
  • Put a capacitor of about 470 µF across VBAT close to the ESCs to absorb voltage spikes.
  • Mount the accelerometer 4 cm from the spin centre, or change IMU_RADIUS in src/main.cpp to match.
  • Assign SBUS channel 8 (index 7 in the code) to a switch on the transmitter. It selects RC or ROS control.
